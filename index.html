Profile

About

I am a Machine Learning Engineer specialising in Speech Technology, with 11+ years of post-PhD research experience. At TCG CREST, my activities span applied research, advancing speech technologies, and postgraduate teaching. I also explore the theoretical foundations of machine learning, particularly information theory, learning algorithms, and inference. My research focuses on improving generalization, explainability, and fairness in machine learning.
md.sahidullah@tcgcrest.org +91-9433289799

Research agenda

Research interests

  • Signal processing and machine learning for acoustic representation learning
  • Voice biometrics: speaker recognition and spoofing countermeasures
  • Audio foundation models and representation learning
  • Audio dataset curation, sampling, and experimental design
  • Speech-based biomarkers for healthcare
