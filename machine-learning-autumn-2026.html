Course overview

This course is jointly taught by Dr. Md Sahidullah and Dr. Avisek Gupta. The portion covered by Dr. Md Sahidullah is primarily based on Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning (PRML).

Course content

  1. Lecture 1: Introduction to the Course
  2. Lecture 2: Introduction (PRML, Chapter 1)
  3. Lecture 3: Probability Distributions (PRML, Chapter 2)
  4. Lecture 4: Mixture Models and EM (PRML, Chapter 9)
  5. Lecture 5: Sampling Methods (PRML, Chapter 12)
  6. Lecture 6: Linear Models for Regression (PRML, Chapter 3)
  7. Lecture 7: Linear Models for Classification (PRML, Chapter 4)
  8. Lecture 8: Evaluation Metrics
  9. Lecture 9: Kernel Methods (PRML, Chapter 6)
  10. Lecture 10: Sparse Kernel Machines (PRML, Chapter 7)
  11. Lecture 11: Graphical Models (PRML, Chapter 8)
  12. Lecture 12: Approximate Inference (PRML, Chapter 10)
  13. Lecture 13: Sampling Methods (PRML, Chapter 11)
  14. Lecture 14: Sequential Data (PRML, Chapter 13)
  15. Lecture 15: Combining Models (PRML, Chapter 14)
